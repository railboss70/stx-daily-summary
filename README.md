# STX Daily Summary

Phone-first Daily Project Summary for STX Railroad Construction supervisors.

Fill the shift report, attach 3–4 job photos, sign, and send a PDF to **reports@stxrailroad.com**.

## Open on your iPhone

Same flow as PrecisionTrack:

1. Open this link in **Safari** (not Chrome):
   https://railboss70.github.io/stx-daily-summary/
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Name it **STX Daily** and Add.

Reports stay on the phone until you send them. Photos and form data are not uploaded to GitHub.

## Crew / equipment

Tap a common class, or tap **Other** and type any machine or extra equipment. Custom names you have used before show up as shortcuts on the next report.

## Office email

Default recipient: `reports@stxrailroad.com` (changeable under Supervisor defaults).
